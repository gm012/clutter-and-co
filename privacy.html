Privacy notice for Clutter & Co. Last updated 13 October 2026.

Who we are and how to reach us

Clutter & Co. provides decluttering and organisation services in Gauteng. For questions about this notice, or to request access, correction or deletion of your information, contact eloise@clutterandco.co.za farryn@clutterandco.co.za or Farryn on 061 525 5757.

What we collect and why

When you submit the questionnaire we collect your name, email, telephone number, suburb, spaces needing attention, contact preferences and the project details you choose to share. We use these to respond, arrange a discussion and prepare a quote. Required fields are needed to handle that request; you can instead call or email us.

We do not use the enquiry form to enrol you in marketing. Please do not submit sensitive personal information or details about children. If we later request photographs, avoid showing people, documents or other private information that is not needed to assess the space.

How information is handled

The questionnaire sends your enquiry to our business mailboxes. Our website hosting and email providers process information needed to deliver and protect these services. The website does not keep a separate enquiry database. Hosting logs can include IP addresses, request times and browser information; temporary security records help limit misuse of the form.

Enquiry details are kept while needed to respond and manage the proposed work. Records relating to accepted work may need to be kept for contractual or legal purposes. Ask us about retention or deletion of a particular enquiry. We restrict handling to people who need the details for the enquiry or service.

Your choices and rights

You may ask what personal information we hold, request corrections, object to processing or ask for deletion where applicable. We may need to verify your identity and explain any legal reason we must retain a record. You may raise a concern with South Africa’s Information Regulator.

Choosing an external WhatsApp or email service brings its provider’s own privacy terms into play. This site has no embedded social feeds, advertising trackers or analytics. Read our cookie notice for essential browser storage.

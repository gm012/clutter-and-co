Cookies, kept simple.

This website uses essential functionality only. There are no optional analytics or advertising cookies.

What is stored

  • Notice preference: local storage named clutter-cookie-notice records the notice version and acknowledgement date for up to six months. It contains no enquiry answers.
  • Questionnaire security: a session cookie named clutter_session connects your form submission to a temporary security token. It is used only when the questionnaire contacts the server and lasts for the browser session.

Your control

There are no optional cookies to accept or reject. You can reopen the notice at any time using the footer’s Privacy & cookies settings control, or clear site data through your browser. Blocking the security session may prevent questionnaire submission; telephone and email remain available.

External services

Fonts and images are served from this website. Clicking a WhatsApp link opens an external service with its own practices. If optional tracking is introduced later, this notice and the controls must be updated before it is enabled.

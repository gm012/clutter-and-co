Simple steps. Lasting calm.

Start with a conversation, then shape a project around your space, priorities and routines.

01 / Tell us about your space

Complete the questionnaire with your contact details, Gauteng suburb, spaces and priorities. You do not need to send private documents or photos at this stage.

02 / Let’s talk

We arrange a phone call to understand what you want to change. We may then request photos or suggest an on-site space discussion. We confirm the walkthrough fee before booking.

03 / Agree the scope

We prepare a project-based package and quote, with the proposed work and any storage or travel costs discussed. Your enquiry does not commit you to a booking.

04 / Sort and organise

We work through the space together, agree what stays and create practical zones and storage systems. Your decisions guide the process.

05 / Make it part of everyday life

We talk through how to use the new layout and keep the system practical for your routines. Any further work is agreed separately.

A few helpful answers

Do you charge by the hour?

We offer project packages. Scope and pricing are agreed after we understand the space.

Is the consultation free?

An on-site space discussion carries a fee. We confirm the amount with you before arranging the visit.

Which areas do you cover?

Our current service area is Gauteng. Give us your suburb so we can confirm availability and travel arrangements.

Do I need to buy containers first?

No, we discuss storage needs once we understand your belongings and the available space.
